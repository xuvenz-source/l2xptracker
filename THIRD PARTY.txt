Third-party components
This application bundles CPython, Tcl/Tk, PyInstaller runtime, NumPy, Pillow,
MSS, pytesseract and their dependencies, plus a locally installed Tesseract OCR
Windows distribution and its language data and dependencies.
Installed dependency license and notice files are copied under Third-party notices;
OCR distribution license/notice files remain in ocr. Review the actual bundled
third-party notices and satisfy the redistribution obligations of the selected
Tesseract Windows distribution (including any source-offer obligations) before
sharing publicly. The builder cannot verify that a local distribution includes
all required notices or that all of its components are redistributable.
